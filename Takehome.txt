Interview Task: Predicting ESc1 Forward Returns 
Objective 
Build a mid-frequency futures strategy that predicts future returns for ESc1 (E-mini S&P 500 front-month contract). 
Data 
5-minute bars containing: 
* 99 anonymized raw signals 
* ESc1 raw prices and volumes/cashflows 
Prices are withheld for the out-of-sample period (last two years of data). 
Scope 
The task has two important components. 
1. Feature Engineering 
Examine the provided features and propose how they should be transformed to be used in the model. In your write-up, explain: 
* How you transform raw signals into model-ready features 
* How you chose transformations and their parameters 
* Any feature selection you performed and the rationale behind it 
* How you evaluated features prior to model fitting 
* The properties you looked for in a good feature, and how you verified them 
2. Modeling 
Fit a model on your features to predict forward returns. In your write-up, discuss: 
* The model(s) you explored and why you selected your final approach 
* How you chose any parameters
* Your evaluation methodology and the model properties you examined 
Deliverables 
1. Report documenting your full approach, results, and reasoning 
2. Code for feature generation, model fitting, and evaluation 
3. Predictions for the out-of-sample period
